Overview

Working across street, landscape, weddings and events.

I shoot both film and digital formats, letting the medium serve the story. Whether documenting quiet landscapes, street scenes caught while travelling, or the once-in-a-lifetime moments of weddings and events, my approach is driven by a single instinct: capture the moment that feels authentically true.

The process is intentionally raw. Film rolls are developed and scanned by hand, so nothing here is masked by a preset — colours are born from real chemistry or captured straight from the sensor, and the natural imperfections stay exactly where they belong.
